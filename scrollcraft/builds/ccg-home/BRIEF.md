# BRIEF: CCG home scroll

> **Authored from the logo deck and the live routes, not from a live interview.** The owner's build prompt said: "Do not interview again. The brief below is the interview, answered from the logo deck and the live routes." The answers below are that prompt's decided answers, kept in its words.

## The eight answers

1. **Vibe:** quiet, exact, blue, black, private. References from the deck only: the emblem on black, the exploded rings, the one-line captions. No other websites.
2. **Journey:** the full mark, then the rings in the deck's picture order, then the existing pages in their current order.
3. **Energy:** quiet, then a rise, then the lock, then quiet.
4. **Feeling:** arrival is calm, separation is curious, the lock is the memory, the rest is trust.
5. **The thing this site does:** the C assembles in the deck's order and each ring is named with the deck's sentence.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal:** stay near premium-minimal. Not brutalist, playful, retro, or dense.
7. **One world or scenes:** the hero is one assembly sequence. Philosophy, why, programs, coach, team, locations, and contact are separate scenes. Not one camera flight through the whole site.
8. **Assets on hand:** the logo PPT, `ccg-mark.svg`, the Meshy emblem FBX and its four maps. No brand film. No approved photo placement (`CLARIFY-002`).

## Grammar and score

Grammar: **typographic poster**, with the emblem as the letterforms (the deck itself calls the parts Letter C, Letter C, Letter G). Why the others lost: filmic one-shot is the single unbroken flight the brief bans; chaptered editorial forbids media above the fold, but the first frame must be the mark; live surface has no product UI to run; continuous world is a camera flight; gallery and split stage have no range or two-sided argument to show; rhythmic cutlist is the opposite of "quiet". Brief overrides: the header and full nav stay (PRODUCT.md), so the grammar's "no persistent nav" is dropped.

| Beat | Device | Why |

| Assembly | `pin` (span 10) + bespoke 3D | the frame holds while the mark changes state; the signature move |
| Vision | `kinetic` lines | one sentence, arriving at poster scale |
| Welcome | `flow` + `in` | a letter reads best still |

Four device families, none twice in a row. Page length about 12.4 viewport-heights across 5 acts.

**Signature move:** each ring is drawn to its word by an elbow leader at the emblem's own 45 degree cut, and the founder's name collapses into the wordmark.

**Reduced motion / no JavaScript / no WebGL:** the still emblem, the wordmark, and the deck sentences as a plain page.

## Change, 17 Oct 2026 (owner request)

"Automate the animation so that users do not have to scroll." The assembly no longer scrubs with scroll. It plays by itself in a single viewport (about 26 seconds, after 1.2 seconds of stillness on the whole mark), rests on the Group line and the people icon, pauses while off screen, and has a Pause / Play / Replay control. The order, the captions, and the lock are unchanged. Reduced motion still shows the still emblem and never autoplays.

Owner also asked for less negative space: the emblem's construction drawing now decorates the hero, the inner pages, the vision, and the close; the home programs show their summaries and facts; the close shows the two offices.
